# After Hours — game development and releases

## Version 1.7.0 — earned Studio commissions and VIP stays

Six successful Studio shoots unlock the four-night high-budget commission.
It has a $350 deposit, a $1,450 base production fee and two vouchers on
successful delivery. The client can license the photos for $80 at settlement
in exchange for one of the delivery vouchers.

Three completed stays with returning guests unlock the three-night VIP
package. New and returning guests can book it. Its $240 base deposit
reserves a suite at $300 per night. An optional farewell costs $60 and
earns an extra $160 at checkout with at least 90/100 satisfaction.

Both booking desks show progress from past careers and preview the terms.
The night report links to each newly earned booking. Existing offline
writing packs still work, with two optional events for the new bookings.

## Version 1.6.2 — recovery during a shoot or guest stay

Taking a financial recovery break partway through a Studio shoot or Resort
stay could prevent the game from reopening the career or restoring its
export. An affected career can now reopen and continue its remaining work.
The Studio, Resort and team roster show the nights actually worked alongside
the new reservation dates. Earlier receipts and payments stay on the nights
they were earned.

## Version 1.6.1 — variable nightly events

Club and team opportunities roll independently with nightly demand. Every
active Studio shoot and Resort stay brings its own decision. Private-room
clients can make up to four additional requests during the same night. Event
times vary, and answering resumes the clock at the selected speed. Opening
a saved night keeps the events and timings already scheduled for that shift.

Night Reports now show the action chosen for promotions, refreshments, team
meals and regulars’ perks, including choices recorded in older reports.

## Version 1.6.0 — staff schedules, Night Reports and earned clips

The team roster shows tonight’s actual assignment, including film leads,
co-stars and Resort hosts, along with each worker’s seven-night schedule.
Night Reports show the revenue each venue received and the costs of the
shift. Each completed shoot or stay shows its return after the deposit
and direct guest costs, and what that earned per staff-night. Older reports
display the detail supported by their recorded figures.

A career keeps a clip it earned if the Library Set or manual photo pairing
later changes. Awards travel with save exports and backups. Playback still
uses the eligible local clip for that character. A new career uses the
current matches to earn its own clips.

## Version 1.5.0 — Studio shoots and Resort stays

Studio and Resort now share the illustrated Club presentation from opening
requirements through active nights. Studio projects offer photo and adult
film briefs, casting, a two-person feature option and on-set decisions.
Choose whether to shoot pickups, take a cover commission, release a Club
publicity preview or deliver an alternate film cut.

Resort hosts match a service plan to a guest’s preferences, handle requests
and can accept a private suite booking for an extra fee. Satisfied guests
can return for a premium. The floor shows current work and the morning
receipt retains the project or stay history. New reservations use these
rules. Previously booked contracts settle on their original terms.

## Version 1.4.1 — department planning and receipts
